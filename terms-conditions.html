2 Nature of Charitable Contributions

All monetary contributions made to Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation are purely voluntary donations made out of compassion and goodwill without any expectation of commercial exchange, goods, services, or personal favors in return.

Donated funds are allocated exclusively toward our registered non-profit charitable activities:

  • Annadaanam: Free satvik food distribution, daily langars, and seasonal bhandaras for slum children and the needy.
  • Gaushala & Animal Welfare: Fodder, veterinary medical treatment, rescue, and shelter for cows and street animals.
  • Child Education: Distribution of educational supplies, notebooks, stationery, and learning support for underprivileged children.
  • Medical Assistance: Free health checkup camps, essential medicines, and patient support.
