Our Mission & Values

Founded with the divine blessings of Lord Shri Ram, the Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation is a registered charitable trust committed to "Service Before Self". We believe that true devotion lies in serving the needy — feeding the hungry, sheltering the helpless, educating the underprivileged, and caring for voiceless animals.

Every initiative of ours is guided by the timeless principles of Dharma (righteousness), Karuna (compassion), and Sewa (service). From grand Bhandaras to the quiet care of a wounded street dog, every act counts in our journey to uplift society.

Message from the Managing Trustee

Trustee 1 of 2

— Dhruva Sharma, Founder & Chairman

"Service to humanity is service to God.

Just stop and think for a moment... How many innocent children are there in this world, whom their own parents have left behind in this cruel world solely to beg or pick rags? They should have books in their tiny hands, but instead, they are forced to wander the streets just to feed their hunger.

And on the other side, there are those elderly parents who spent their entire youth raising and nurturing their children, and when the time came for those children to be the staff of their old age, the very same children showed them the door. Sobbing on street corners or in old-age homes, those parents live a life of everyday suffocation.

Witnessing these two tales of pain, Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation has come into being. Our resolve is not merely to perform service on paper, but to place a pen in the hands of those helpless children and to provide a respectful lap and a family to those discarded parents.

This path is difficult, and we are just beginning this journey. But with your support, no child will be forced to beg, and no parent will be left homeless. Come, be our companions in this sacred and compassionate journey. May Lord Shri Ram give us all the strength to fulfill this noble mission."

Message from the Managing Trustee

Trustee 2 of 2

— Vaibhav Sharma, Founder & Co-Chairman

"True dharma is illuminated when we dedicate our lives to the selfless service of living beings. As taught in our sacred scriptures, 'Parhit Saris Dharam Nahi Bhai' — there is no greater spiritual devotion than wiping away someone's tears.

At Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation, our mission is driven by a deep sense of responsibility towards humanity and nature. Through continuous Annadaanam, we strive to ensure that no hungry soul is turned away. In our Gaushala Sewa, we revere and shelter Gau Mata with devotion, and through educational & medical assistance, we strive to build an equitable, self-reliant future for underprivileged families.

No noble revolution is created by an individual alone; it is born from the collective compassion of society. Every contribution, whether of time, prayer, or resources, becomes an integral part of this ongoing Mahayajna. Our Key Initiatives

Four pillars of our social welfare work — each driven by devotion and executed with sincerity

Annadaanam

Free Food Distribution

"No one should go to bed hungry" — with this strong resolve, we have embarked on our Annadaanam journey. Although we are in our initial phase right now, our vision and commitment are vast. We are steadily building our capacity to ensure that in the near future, nutritious satvik meals reach thousands of underprivileged individuals, slum children, and needy souls on a regular basis. Initiating Regular Food Distribution (Initial Phase) Upcoming Seasonal & Special Bhandara Events Future Mobile Vans for Slum Areas

  • Daily Langar for 500+ people
  • Seasonal Bhandara Events
  • Mobile Vans for Slums

Gaushala & Animal Welfare

Care for Cows & Strays

"Service to voiceless beings is true service to the Divine." Although we are just beginning this journey, our resolve is to provide safety and love to every abandoned cow and stray animal on the streets. With our limited resources, we are taking small yet strong steps toward tending to injured animals and providing them with shelter. Initial care and fodder service for abandoned voiceless animals Rescue efforts for injured and sick animals Future expansion of Gaushala and veterinary camps

  • 200+ Cows in Gaushala
  • Rescue & Ambulance Service
  • Free Veterinary Camps

For Underprivileged Kids

Stepping Toward a Brighter Future for Underprivileged Children Education is every child's right. No child should be deprived of learning due to a lack of resources—that is our core dream. Right now, we are taking small, dedicated steps to bring the light of education to slum areas and needy children. Free initial teaching and educational support for underprivileged children Distribution of books, notebooks, and learning materials Continuous community outreach to encourage education Educational Trips & Learning Tours (Organizing excursions to broaden their horizons and spark curiosity)
