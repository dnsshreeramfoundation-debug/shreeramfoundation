1 Introduction & Scope

At Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation ("we", "our", "us", or "the Foundation"), we revere the sacred trust placed in us by our donors, volunteers, well-wishers, and beneficiaries. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of transparency and protecting the personal privacy of anyone who visits our website or interacts with our charitable initiatives.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is safeguarded, and the rights you hold concerning your personal data. By accessing our website, donating, or contacting us, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

When you voluntarily make a donation, request an 80G tax receipt, fill out our contact form, or reach out to us via WhatsApp/Email, we may collect:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile/WhatsApp Phone Number, and Mailing Address.
  • Tax Compliance Details: Permanent Account Number (PAN Card) — mandatory under Section 80G(5) and Rule 18AB of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for issuing Form 10BE donation tax exemption certificates.
  • Donation Transaction Information: Amount donated, date and time of donation, chosen payment mode (UPI, NEFT/RTGS, IMPS), and Bank UTR/Transaction Reference ID. (Note: We do not collect or store your ATM PIN, Net Banking passwords, or CVV numbers.)
  • Cause Preference: Your selected seva domain (Annadaanam, Gaushala Sewa, Child Education, Medical Camps, or General Fund).

3 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for non-profit and charitable governance purposes, including:

  • Generating, issuing, and delivering official donation receipts and 80G tax exemption certificates.
  • Filing mandatory statutory returns with the Income Tax Department (Form 10BD / 10BE) and Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
  • Communicating verification updates, payment acknowledgments, and impact reports regarding our ongoing food distributions and cow protection programs.
  • Answering your inquiries, support requests, or grievance submissions.

4 No Commercial Sale or Rental of Data

We never sell, rent, trade, or commercially monetize donor lists or personal details under any circumstances.

Your data is accessed only by authorized trustees and accounts officers of Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation solely to process official charitable records. It may be disclosed to government bodies (such as the Income Tax Department or Registrar of Companies) only when legally required by law.

5 Security & Data Protection

We implement industry-standard organizational and technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of your personal information. Our official bank transactions are executed via secure banking infrastructure provided by AU Small Finance Bank and official NPCI UPI protocols.
