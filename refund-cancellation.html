Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear and transparent policies regarding charitable donations and technical error corrections

Donations Are Non-Refundable (एक बार दान होने के बाद रिफंड नहीं होगा)

Charitable donations once made voluntarily to Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation are strictly non-refundable and non-cancellable under normal circumstances, as all funds are immediately utilized for ongoing social welfare causes (daily Langar Annadaanam, Gau Seva fodder, child education supplies, and medical assistance).

1 General Non-Refundability Policy

Dashrathnandan Shree Ram Foundation is a registered Section 8 non-profit organization under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN: U88900RJ2026NPL112929).

As a matter of trust policy and statutory financial compliance, all donations made voluntarily through our website, bank transfer, UPI QR code, or direct payments are final. We do not facilitate voluntary change-of-mind cancellations or requests for refunds after a donation has been processed.

2 Exception Cases: Technical Errors & Duplicate Deductions

Refunds are entertained exclusively in genuine cases of banking, payment gateway, or technical errors. A refund request will be considered only under the following specific circumstances:

Duplicate Deduction

Your account was debited multiple times for a single donation transaction due to server lag or network disruption.

Excess / Over-Deduction

An amount higher than your entered donation value was deducted as a result of a verified technical glitch.

Unauthorized Misuse

Fraudulent payment made through compromised credentials, verified with an official police/bank dispute report.

3 How to Claim a Technical Error Refund

If you have experienced a technical error or duplicate deduction, you must notify our trust accounts office within 7 days from the transaction date. Please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to dns.shreeramfoundation@gmail.com or a WhatsApp message to +91 95496 23048 with the subject line "Refund Request - Technical Error".
  2. Provide the following mandatory verification documents:
    • Donor Full Name & Mobile Number
    • Transaction UTR Number / Reference ID
    • Bank statement or screenshot clearly showing the duplicate/excess deduction
    • Date and exact amount debited

4 Refund Processing & Timeline

  • Verification Period: Our finance team will review and reconcile the transaction with AU Small Finance Bank records within 3 to 5 working days.
  • Payment Reversal: Upon successful verification of duplicate or excess credit, the refund will be initiated directly to the original source bank account / UPI ID from which the funds originated within 7 to 10 working days.
  • Under no circumstances will refunds be processed in cash or to an unverified third-party account.

5 Impact on 80G Tax Exemption Certificates

If an 80G tax receipt has already been generated for a refunded transaction, the receipt shall stand null and void. In case the donation details have already been submitted to the Income Tax Department under Form 10BD, a corresponding revision/rectification will be filed as per CBDT guidelines.
